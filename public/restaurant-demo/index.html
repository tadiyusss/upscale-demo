The Restaurant

One hearth. Forty seats.
A menu that follows the seasons.

There is no gas in our kitchen — only oak, embers, and whatever our growers brought us this week. The menu is short because attention should be spent on cooking, not printing.

Our Story

Born from a wood oven and a very short menu.

In 2019, chef Daniel Reyes and Mara Ellison took over an old print shop on Marlow Street, knocked a hole in the roof for a flue, and lit the oven they still cook on today.

Six years on, the fire hasn’t gone out — it goes down overnight and wakes up with us at ten each morning.
