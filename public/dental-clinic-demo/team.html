Meet our dentists

Meet the people behind your care.

Three dentists with different specialties — and one shared standard of patient-first care.

Founder & Lead Dentist

Dr. Eleanor Vance, DDS

General & restorative dentistry · Implant treatment planning

  • DDM, University of the Philippines Manila
  • Member, Philippine Dental Association
  • Advanced training in implant treatment planning

Dr. Vance spent her first decade in dentistry inside large group practices, where she watched ten-minute appointments erode both patient trust and clinical quality. In 2016 she opened Aurelia to prove a practice could work the other way around: longer visits, written plans, and continuity — the same dentist who plans your treatment is the one who carries it out.

Her clinical focus spans comprehensive general care, restorative work, and the meticulous planning behind our implant program. Long-standing patients know her for explaining everything twice if needed, and never once making them feel rushed.

"I want every patient to leave understanding their own mouth better than when they arrived."

How Dr. Vance cares for patients

Unhurried examinations, plain-language explanations, and a strong preference for preserving natural tooth structure wherever it's clinically sound to do so.

Associate Dentist

Dr. Omar Haddad, DDS

Restorative & cosmetic dentistry · Aesthetic treatment planning

  • DDM, Centro Escolar University
  • Member, Philippine Dental Association
  • Advanced training in aesthetic restorative techniques

Dr. Haddad is the clinician patients ask for when appearance matters. His restorative work — crowns, bonding, veneers — is planned with an almost architectural attention to shade, shape and proportion, always anchored in what will function well for years, not just photograph well for a week.

He joined Aurelia after several years in a busy city practice and quickly became known for his calm chairside manner, particularly with patients who feel self-conscious about their smiles.

"Good cosmetic dentistry should be quiet. People should notice you look well — not notice your teeth were done."

How Dr. Haddad cares for patients

Detailed mock-ups before permanent work begins, honest conversations about what aesthetics can realistically achieve, and conservative preparations that protect healthy enamel.

Orthodontist

Dr. Daniel Reyes, DDM

Orthodontics · Clear aligner therapy · Pediatric-friendly care

  • DDM, University of the East
  • Certificate in Orthodontics
  • Member, Association of Philippine Orthodontists

Dr. Reyes leads all orthodontic treatment at the studio, from teen comprehensive braces to adult clear-aligner cases. He is a firm believer that alignment is health care first: properly aligned teeth are cleaner, wear more evenly, and support comfortable bites for decades.

Families particularly value his patience with younger patients — he explains each stage so children know exactly what is happening and why, which turns anxious first visits into routine ones.

"Orthodontics is a long journey. My job is to make it predictable, comfortable, and worth it at the end."

How Dr. Reyes cares for patients

Honest suitability assessments — including telling people when aligners aren't right for them — regular progress reviews, and retainers planned from day one so results hold.

What unites us

Three standards every dentist here practices by

  1. Explain before we treat

    No procedure starts until you understand what it involves, why it's recommended, and what alternatives exist.

  2. Treat conservatively

    We preserve healthy tooth structure aggressively and replace it reluctantly — the least intervention that solves the problem.

  3. Stay consistent

    The dentist who knows your history is the one who treats you. Continuity is a clinical advantage, not a luxury.
