Dental services

Complete care for your smile.

From routine check-ups to implants and orthodontics — explore the treatments we offer.

Our treatment philosophy

The right treatment is the least treatment that solves the problem

Before recommending any procedure, we ask two questions: what will genuinely help this patient, and what can reasonably wait? Preventive care comes first; cosmetic work is planned around healthy foundations; and every option is presented with its trade-offs, so you can make an informed decision rather than a pressured one.

01 · Everyday care

General Dentistry

General dentistry is the foundation of everything else we do. Regular examinations and professional cleaning keep your mouth healthy and catch small issues — early decay, gum inflammation, worn enamel — long before they become painful or expensive.

  • Comprehensive exams & digital x-rays
  • Professional cleanings & hygiene visits
  • Tooth-coloured fillings
  • Gum health & periodontal care
  • Root canal therapy
  • Crowns & bridges
Who it's for Everyone, ideally twice a year. If it has been more than two years since your last visit, you're welcome here too — we'll simply take things one careful step at a time.

02 · Confidence

Cosmetic Dentistry

Cosmetic treatment aims for results people notice without knowing why. We plan every case around your facial proportions and existing tooth character — the goal is a refined version of your own smile, not an identical copy of someone else's.

  • Porcelain veneers
  • Composite bonding
  • Tooth-coloured reshaping
  • Aesthetic crowns & replacement
  • Smile design consultation
  • Combined whitening plans
Honest expectations Before any aesthetic work begins, we make sure underlying health is settled first — cosmetic treatment on untreated decay or gum disease doesn't last. We'll show you previews where possible and discuss what is realistically achievable for your teeth.

03 · Restoration

Dental Implants

A dental implant replaces a missing tooth with a titanium post and a custom crown, restoring both function and appearance. Implants are planned meticulously at our studio — imaging, positioning and healing time are all mapped before anything begins.

  • Single-tooth implants
  • Implant-supported bridges
  • Consultation & 3D planning
  • Guided placement
  • Healing abutments & crowns
  • Long-term implant maintenance
The process, briefly Planning and imaging → implant placement under local anaesthetic → a healing period of several months → final crown fitting. Suitability depends on bone volume and general health, which we assess carefully before recommending this route.

04 · Alignment

Orthodontics

Straightening teeth isn't only about appearance — aligned teeth are easier to clean and distribute biting forces more evenly. Under Dr. Reyes we offer both fixed braces and clear aligners, selected after a proper assessment of your bite rather than your preference alone.

  • Clear aligner therapy
  • Fixed metal & ceramic braces
  • Bite assessment & records
  • Retainers after treatment
  • Teen & adult treatment
  • Regular progress reviews
Patient considerations Orthodontic treatment typically takes months to a few years depending on complexity, with regular review visits throughout. Aligners require consistent daily wear to work as intended — we'll tell you honestly if they're not the right tool for your case.

05 · Brightness

Teeth Whitening

Professional whitening lightens the shade of natural teeth safely and predictably. We offer supervised take-home trays custom-fitted to your teeth, which deliver gradual, even results over one to two weeks.

  • Shade assessment & records
  • Custom take-home trays
  • Supervised gel programme
  • Sensitivity guidance
  • Top-up treatments
  • Maintenance advice
What to expect Whitening works on natural enamel only — existing fillings, crowns and veneers won't change colour, which we factor into your plan. Results vary by individual; some deep staining responds better to other treatments, and we'll say so if that's the case.

06 · Urgent support

Emergency Care

Dental pain rarely arrives at a convenient time. We hold time in our schedule each day for urgent cases, and existing patients can reach our out-of-hours line for guidance on anything that can't wait until morning.

  • Severe toothache
  • Chipped or broken teeth
  • Knocked-out teeth
  • Lost fillings or crowns
  • Dental abscesses
  • Sports injuries
If this is a medical emergency For facial swelling affecting breathing or swallowing, or any injury involving the head or neck, call 911 or go to the nearest hospital emergency room first — these need urgent medical attention, not a dental appointment.
